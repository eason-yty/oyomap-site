Privacy Policy

Private by design, clear by default.

Your photos, videos, stories, precise locations, and face-analysis results are processed on your device. Oyomap does not upload that content to its own servers.

Effective October 1, 2026

The short version: Oyomap is local-first. We do not sell personal information, use cross-app tracking, or use your memories for advertising. Optional Firebase usage analytics starts only after you explicitly agree to share analytics. Limited crash diagnostics run separately to help keep the app reliable.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Oyomap ("Oyomap," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Oyomap iOS app, this website, or contact support.

2. Information processed on your device

With the permissions you choose to grant, Oyomap may process the following locally on your Apple device:

  • photos, videos, and Live Photos selected from your photo library;
  • PhotoKit asset identifiers and media metadata, including capture date and embedded location;
  • stories, captions, friend profiles, app settings, and autoplay selections;
  • face crops, numerical face embeddings, visual feature data, and scene classifications used to organize memories; and
  • cached images, map state, and other data needed to display and play your stories.

This content is stored in the app's local container or referenced from your Photos library. Oyomap does not operate a user account, cloud photo library, or custom server that receives this content.

4. How we use information

We use information only to provide and improve Oyomap, maintain security and reliability, process purchases through Apple, answer support requests, comply with law, and protect our users and rights.

5. When information is shared

We share limited information with service providers only as needed to operate Oyomap: Google Firebase for optional analytics, crash reporting, and configuration; Apple for Photos, maps, geocoding, and purchases; and Asana if you choose to submit a hosted feedback form. Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

We may also disclose information if legally required or necessary to protect users, Oyomap, or others. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use third-party advertising SDKs.

6. Retention

  • On-device content: retained until you delete the relevant content or uninstall Oyomap, subject to copies that remain in your Photos library or device backups.
  • Analytics: identifiable event-level data is retained for no longer than 14 months. Aggregated reports that no longer identify an individual installation may remain longer.
  • Crash diagnostics: retained only as long as reasonably needed to investigate reliability and subject to Firebase's service controls.
  • Support and feedback: retained while needed to answer your request and for reasonable product and legal recordkeeping, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Choose whether to share optional usage analytics, and change your choice at any time using Share Usage Analytics in Oyomap Settings. Crash reporting is handled separately.
  • Use iOS Settings to grant full, limited, or no Photos access and to change that choice later.
  • Delete stories and friend profiles in the app, or uninstall Oyomap to remove its local app container.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account's subscription settings.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or other rights available under applicable privacy law.

Because Oyomap has no user account and keeps memory content on your device, we may not possess or be able to identify that local content. Deleting the app does not delete originals from your Photos library.

8. Children

Oyomap is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a user account. If you believe a child has provided information through support or feedback, contact us so we can address it.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process limited service data in countries other than where you live. Those providers use their own legal safeguards for international transfers.

10. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to limit the data collected and protect information. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Oyomap changes. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app where appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@oyomap.com. For product help, visit Oyomap Support.
