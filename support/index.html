OyoMap Support

Let’s get you back to your memories.

Start with the quick answers below. If something still is not working, tell us what happened and we will help as soon as practical.

Email support@oyomap.com

Before you email

Please include your OyoMap app version, iOS version, device model, what you expected, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful when they do not reveal private content.

Please do not send original photos or videos, face crops, precise coordinates, PhotoKit asset identifiers, full purchase receipts, or other sensitive personal information. We will ask for a safer diagnostic if one is needed.

Quick answers

Why can’t OyoMap see all of my photos?

Open iOS Settings → Apps → OyoMap → Photos. You can grant Full Access or add the missing items to Limited Access. OyoMap will not silently remove inaccessible photos from an existing story.

Why is a photo or video still loading?

If the original is stored in iCloud, iOS may need to download it. Keep the device online and OyoMap in the foreground, then try again. Network conditions and iCloud availability can affect the wait.

What if a photo has no place or date?

OyoMap can only place a memory on the map when the item includes usable location information. A missing or edited capture date may also affect its position in a story. Your original item remains unchanged.

What happens if a photo used in a story is deleted?

OyoMap may show that the original is unavailable instead of silently removing it. Check Recently Deleted in Photos if the deletion was accidental, or edit the story to use another memory.

Why did a Story or Friend Film export stop?

Keep OyoMap in the foreground, make sure the device has enough free storage, and allow iCloud originals to finish downloading. Try a shorter export if the issue continues, then include the media mix and the step where it stopped when contacting support.

How do I restore Oyo Pro?

Open the Oyo Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. You must be signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. You can also view the subscription under iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Apple manages billing, cancellations, and refunds. Manage a subscription in your Apple Account settings. For refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn optional analytics off?

Open OyoMap Settings and turn off Share Analytics. This stops future optional product-analytics collection and does not affect core features. Crash diagnostics used for app reliability are separate. See the Privacy choices section.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual stories or friend profiles inside OyoMap. Uninstalling the app removes its local app container, subject to device backups. Your original photos and videos remain in your Photos library unless you delete them there.

Does OyoMap upload my photos or face data?

No. OyoMap processes memories and face-analysis results on your device and does not upload that content to an OyoMap server. See the Privacy Policy for the limited service data handled by Apple and Firebase.

Privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@oyomap.com. Because memory content is local and OyoMap has no user account, we may not possess or be able to identify content stored only on your device.
